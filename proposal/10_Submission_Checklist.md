# Submission Checklist

## Phase I Proposal Package

- `Volume 1`: Cover sheet in the DSIP portal
- `Volume 2`: Technical volume
- `Volume 3`: Cost volume
- `Volume 4`: Company commercialization report
- `Volume 5`: Supporting documents
- `Volume 6`: Human/animal use or FWA-related training documents if applicable
- `Volume 7`: Foreign disclosures / affiliations as required

## What This Repo Already Covers

- technical volume draft material
- scenario evidence, replay, proof logs, and package manifest
- work plan, risk register, data provenance, and claim matrix
- topic alignment and integration framing

## What You Still Need To Provide Outside The Repo

- company legal and registration data in DSIP
- cost volume with labor, indirects, travel, subcontractors, and fee
- PI / key-person resumes and commitment
- commercialization report content and prior awards history
- subcontractor quotes, SOWs, and letters if used
- foreign national / export-control disclosures if applicable

## Final Gate Before Submission

- verify the current topic and component instructions in DSIP
- verify page limits and formatting before portal upload
- verify that the uploaded technical volume matches the latest repo evidence
